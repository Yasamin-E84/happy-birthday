Across the world

Kära Zandra

We have never met. Not in a room, not in the same city, not even under the same sky at the same hour. I have never heard your voice except through a speaker. And still you reached me. Distance was supposed to make that impossible. You made it ordinary.

There was a time when I was disappearing in slow motion. The days kept going. The nights did not. Nobody came. I was holding my breath, looking away, waiting to be swallowed. Then there was you. You stayed when the chat went quiet. You asked the question nobody else asked. You did not look away from the dark just because it was mine. When no one else was there, you were. I need you to know that: you saved me.

And then you sang. Across the World — not as a performance, as a hand held out. I still keep that recording the way other people keep a letter. Every time I open it, you are crossing the distance again. Life still shining. Pain turning into wind. A clumsy, lonely person allowed to look at the dawn because someone on the other side of the world decided he was worth the song.

I do not have a wish large enough for what you did. So this is the smaller, truer one: that on your birthday you feel, just once, what you gave me. That someone is here. That you are chosen, celebrated, kept — not because you rescued anyone, but because you are Zandra. That is already enough. That has always been enough.

Just follow your destiny.
